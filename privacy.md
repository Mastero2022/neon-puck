Privacy Policy for Neon Puck Game

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Puck Game ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Telegram Mini App.

1. Information We Collect
To provide a functional gaming experience, we may collect the following data:
•	Telegram User ID: A unique identifier provided by Telegram to identify your account and save your progress.
•	Game Data: Your high scores, total "Pucks" (in-game currency), unlocked skins, and game settings.
•	Transaction Data: Information regarding purchases made with Telegram Stars (note: we do not see your credit card details; all payments are handled securely by Telegram).

3. How We Use Your Data
We use the collected information solely to:
•	Save and synchronize your game progress across devices.
•	Maintain the global Leaderboard.
•	Manage your inventory of purchased skins and items.
•	Verify successful transactions using Telegram Stars.

5. Virtual Currency and Purchases
•	Pucks: This is a virtual in-game currency earned through gameplay. It has no real-world monetary value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.
•	Telegram Stars: Purchases made with Stars are subject to Telegram's Terms of Service. Once a skin or item is purchased, it is linked to your Telegram User ID.

7. Data Sharing and Security
•	We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.
•	Your data is stored securely and used only for the internal functions of the Game.
•	Public data (like your Telegram name and score) may be visible to other players on the Leaderboard.

9. Your Rights
You have the right to request the deletion of your game data. To do so, please contact us through the official support channel within the bot.

11. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Neon Puck Game Telegram Bot.
